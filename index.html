How Amazon FBA fees are calculated in 2026 (fulfillment, referral, storage)

This calculator uses the 2026 US rate card: fulfillment fees by weight bracket, category (standard / apparel / hazmat) and price band (under $9.90 / $9.90–$50 / over $50); size tiers standard, large bulky and oversize; a dimensional weight divisor of 139 in³/lb; monthly storage of $0.78/cu ft (standard, off-peak) or $2.40/cu ft in Q4 peak; a 3.5% fuel & logistics surcharge on fulfillment (effective 2026-04-17); the average $0.32 peak fulfillment surcharge for shipments arriving Oct 15, 2026 – Jan 14, 2027; and the return processing cost (referral fee × 20%) that sellers pay when a customer returns an item.

FAQ

Does Amazon charge storage per cubic foot? Yes — monthly storage is charged per cubic foot, and the rate roughly triples in Q4 for standard-size items ($0.78 → $2.40 in this 2026 rate set), so Q4 storage can quietly erase margin on bulky, slow-moving products.

What is billable weight? Amazon charges fulfillment on the greater of actual weight and dimensional weight (L × W × H ÷ 139 for US standard sizes). Light but bulky products pay on dimensional weight.

What referral fee does Amazon take? Most categories pay 15% of the sale price (minimum $0.30). This calculator defaults to 15% — adjust it for your category.

What does a returned unit cost me? Amazon keeps 20% of the referral fee as a refund processing fee — on a 15% referral that's 3% of the sale price, which this calculator adds as a per-unit line item.

Is this an official Amazon tool? No — it's an independent estimator. Fees are estimates; final charges appear in Seller Central reports.
